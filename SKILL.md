# EDP AI Initiatives Status Skill

## Capabilities

- Greet EDP executives and introduce the AI initiative review
- Present the full portfolio overview with status and progress
- Drill into SAP Autonomous Enterprise or Joule for Consultants
- Highlight risks and items needing executive attention

## Example Phrases

- **"Hello Joule and welcome to EDP"** ← opening greeting
- "Show me the AI initiative status"
- "Tell me about SAP Autonomous Enterprise"
- "What is the status of Joule for Consultants?"
- "What needs executive attention?"
- "Which initiatives are at risk?"
